About Me

From Avionics to AI

I started my career maintaining aircraft electronics in the United States Marine Corps, where I developed the systematic troubleshooting mindset that still drives my engineering approach today.

Since then, I've designed safety-critical systems that literally saved lives - from automating deadly oil rig operations to building redundant crane monitoring systems that prevent structural failures at Boeing facilities.

Today, I'm pioneering AI-assisted development practices, proving that teams can achieve orders of magnitude greater velocity while maintaining code quality. I completed my BS in Software Engineering at age 40 while working full-time - I believe in lifelong learning and continuous improvement.
